Our History & Heritage

Founded with a vision to make practical computing education accessible and affordable, TECH_INSTITUTE began as a modest evening coding and computer literacy center with just two instructors and twenty desktop workstations.

Over the years, growing demand from local businesses and startups inspired us to expand into a full-fledged technical training institute offering accredited programs in Full-Stack Software Engineering, Cloud Networking, Data Science, and Ethical Hacking.

Today, our alumni build digital solutions across banking, telecommunications, fintech, and public service, supported by modern computer labs, industry mentors, and seamless mobile fee payment systems.

Key Milestones

Phase 1 — The Foundation

Launched foundational ICDL, hardware maintenance, and office productivity bootcamps to bridge the local digital divide.

Phase 2 — Software & Networking Labs

Opened dedicated high-speed Linux, Python/Django, and Cisco networking laboratories for hands-on project training.

Phase 3 — Digital Campus & M-Pesa Integration

Introduced our unified Student & Instructor Portal featuring online applications, instant M-Pesa fee clearance, and digital transcripts.
